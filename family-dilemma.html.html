A Private Moment Turned Into an Unexpected Family Dilemma

I've been fantasizing about my future Son-in-law after I accidentally saw him naked in the shower.

Ok so I (48f) am married to the love of my life and we’ll be celebrating 30 years in March. I also have a 27 year old daughter and she has a fiancé of the same age, and they both are living with us right now. We all have a great relationship and we’re all family.

Last week my future SIL and husband were both getting ready to go somewhere and my husband said he’d shower after our FSIL. I was downstairs and heard someone get out of the shower and someone else go in, and I needed to do some things to fix my hair so I went to the bathroom to use my hairdryer and such.

I walked in without knocking thinking my husband was the one showering, but somewhere in the course of events they decided to switch places and my FSIL was standing there naked. I closed the door immediately and told him I was sorry, and we talked about it after and he said it was fine and despite some awkwardness, we got over it.

That being said…I keep thinking about his body. For the past week every time I’ve showered, I’ve fantasized about him coming in and having sex with me (and I almost always hope/wish it happens).

My husband and I have a wonderful relationship, but we haven’t been physically intimate with each other in a while. I just feel so gross having this attraction towards a future family member and I feel like I’m seriously betraying my husband and daughter.

So yeah. I haven’t told anyone about this and I feel shitty and wanted to get this off my chest.
